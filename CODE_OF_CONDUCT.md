# Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in the AVA project to be respectful, constructive, and
safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful when discussing ideas, code, and people.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Respect differing viewpoints and protect other people's privacy.
- Focus discussion on the project's work and documented evidence.
- Follow the repository's security reporting guidance; do not publish
  vulnerability details or personal information that could put someone at risk.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, targeted insults, unwanted
sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and retaliation for raising a
concern are not acceptable. Disagreement, good-faith criticism, and reporting a
security issue are not violations by themselves.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove violating content, ask a participant
to stop, temporarily restrict participation, or block access when warranted.
Actions should be proportionate, based on the available evidence, and recorded
with restricted details where privacy requires it. There is no automatic
punishment or retaliation system.

## Reporting and appeals

Report a conduct concern privately to a repository owner through GitHub. Do not
use a public issue for sensitive personal details. Include relevant links,
approximate dates, and the outcome sought. Maintainers should acknowledge a
report within 3 business days where practicable, review it impartially, and
share the decision with involved parties on a need-to-know basis.

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request an appeal by replying
privately to the decision-maker, explaining why the decision should be
reconsidered. Where possible, an owner not involved in the initial decision
should review the appeal. Retaliation against a reporter or appeal participant
is prohibited.
